Learn About Us

Finance Analyzer helps small business entrepreneurs evaluate financial performance and benchmark against industry peers.

Users enter financial statement data to get insights into profitability, operational efficiency, and cash flow.

The application compares selected metrics with S&P 500 industry peers.

How It Works

→ Evaluate Your Organization’s Financial Performance

  • Compare profitability, efficiency, and cash flow with industry peers.

→Have available:

  1. Income statement
  2. Balance sheet
  3. Statement of cash flows

Classification Categories:

  1. Industry Leader
    • Strong revenue scale, profitability, and operational efficiency
  2. Efficient but Under-Scaled
    • Strong profitability but smaller market scale than competitors.
  3. Margin Strong but Operational Weak
    • Healthy margins but weaker operating performance relative to peers
  4. Scale Without Efficiency
    • Strong revenue generation but lower profitability and efficiency.
  5. Underperformer
    • Trails industry peers across multiple financial metrics.

Metrics Calculated:

      Gross Profit
      Net Income
      Net Income Margin
      EBITDA
      Operating Cash Flow
      Free Cash Flow
      Working Capital
